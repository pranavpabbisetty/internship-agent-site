Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

internship-agent is a personal tool. It is used only by its owner, Pranav Pabbisetty, and is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

The app reads the owner's own Gmail account with read-only access (the gmail.readonly scope). It uses this access only to classify job-application emails as confirmation, assessment, interview, rejection or offer.

What it stores

For each job-application email it stores only the sender's domain, the subject, the date and the classification. This information is stored locally on the owner's computer. Email bodies are not stored.

What it never does

The app never sends, deletes or modifies email. No data is sold or shared with third parties.
